The shop is closed. The spreadsheets are still open.

QuietOps turns inventory, sales, delivery and invoice files into a short, source-linked review queue. Deterministic calculations flag issues; Ministral 3 8B explains the supplied evidence through local Ollama. The owner reviews a draft and approves it for manual export.
